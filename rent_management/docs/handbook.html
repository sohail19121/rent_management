Chapter 01 · Getting started

The system at a glance

Rent Management keeps track of your buildings, the units inside them and the tenants who rent them. From the lease it bills rent automatically every period, follows up on payments, and handles the deposit and the final settlement when a tenant leaves.

The building blocks

PropertyA building or complex
→
UnitA flat, room, shop, office or warehouse in it
→
LeaseAgreement between a tenant and a unit, with dates and rent
→
InvoicesRent, deposit, electricity, late fees, repairs
→
PaymentsRecorded by you, or paid online by the tenant

The lease sits at the centre. Once you activate it, the system does this on its own:

  • creates the rent invoice before every due date and emails it to the tenant,
  • sends a reminder before the due date and a notice when the payment is late,
  • adds a late fee after the grace days you allowed,
  • increases the rent on its escalation date, if you set one,
  • prepares the monthly electricity reading for metered units,
  • warns you and the tenant when the lease is about to end, and closes it once the end date has passed.

The unit status (Vacant, Reserved, Occupied) follows the lease. You never have to update it by hand.

Who uses what

Your staff (back office)

  • Work in the Rent Management app inside Odoo.
  • Create properties, units, tenants and leases.
  • Record payments, handle repairs, settle move-outs.

Your tenants (portal)

  • Sign in on the website with their own login.
  • See their lease, invoices and payments, and pay online.
  • Report repairs with photos and follow their progress.
The Rent Management app works on top of Odoo Accounting. Every invoice the app creates is a normal Odoo customer invoice, so your accountant sees them in Accounting as well, with all the usual tools (payments, bank reconciliation, credit notes, tax reports).
Chapter 02 · Getting started

A full year, step by step

This chapter follows one tenant through a whole year, so you can see what you do and what the system does by itself, in the order it happens.

The example: Sunrise Apartments, unit A-101, rented to Ravi Kumar. Rent ₹15,000 per month, due on the 1st. Security deposit ₹30,000. Late fee ₹500 after 5 grace days. Electricity metered at ₹8 per kWh. Lease from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. All settings are left at their defaults (amounts are in your company currency).

You = a step you take. Automatic = the system does it overnight.

25 MarchYou

Create the lease for A-101 and Ravi (chapter 10). Choosing the unit fills in the rent, deposit and maintenance charge from the unit. The unit has a meter number, so electricity is set to Metered. Click Confirm. Ravi gets the Lease Confirmation email with the lease agreement PDF attached, and the unit becomes Reserved.

25 MarchYou (accountant)

Click Invoice Deposit. A ₹30,000 deposit invoice is created. When Ravi pays, register the payment (chapter 15) and the deposit status becomes Received.

1 AprilYou

Ravi moves in. Click Activate (Move-in). The lease becomes Active, the unit Occupied, and the first billing date is set to 1 April.

Night of 1 AprilAutomatic

The April rent invoice (₹15,000, due 1 April) is created, posted and emailed to Ravi with the invoice PDF. The next billing date moves to 1 May.

26 AprilAutomatic

Invoices are created 5 days ahead, so the May rent invoice (due 1 May) is created and emailed today.

28 AprilAutomatic

The May invoice is due in 3 days and still unpaid, so Ravi receives the Payment Reminder email.

1 MayAutomatic

April has ended, so an electricity reading for 1–30 April is created with status To Enter. The property manager gets a to-do: "Enter the electricity meter reading".

2 MayYou

The manager scans the QR label on A-101's meter with their phone, types the reading (say 4,520), optionally takes a photo, and saves. The reading is Confirmed (chapter 18).

2 MayAutomatic

The May invoice is now overdue, so Ravi receives the Overdue Notice email (once per invoice).

7 MayAutomatic

Still unpaid after the 5 grace days, so a separate ₹500 Late Fee invoice is created and noted on the lease (chapter 16).

8 MayYou (accountant)

Ravi pays by bank transfer. Open the invoice and click Register Payment. The invoice shows Paid. If you need one, print a Rent Receipt.

27 MayAutomatic

The June invoice is created. It contains the June rent plus the April electricity reading (electricity is billed one month behind, together with the next rent).

June → February

The same cycle repeats every month: invoice, reminder, reading, payment.

1 March 2027Automatic

The lease ends within 30 days, so it becomes Expiring. Ravi gets the Lease Expiry Notice email, and the manager gets a to-do: "Lease ending: renew or plan move-out".

March 2027You

Either click Renew to prepare the next term (chapter 12), or, when Ravi leaves, click Terminate / Move-out and settle the deposit (chapter 13).

1 April 2027Automatic

If nothing was done, the end date has passed and the lease becomes Expired. If a renewal is in place, it becomes Renewed instead.

Over the whole year, you only had to create and activate the lease, enter the meter readings, record payments and decide what happens at the end. The system took care of everything else.

Each staff member gets one of three Rent Management roles. Each role includes everything the role above it can do.

Giving a staff member access

  1. Go to Settings›Users & Companies›Users and open the user (or click New).
  2. In the access rights, find Rent Management and pick Property Manager, Rent Accountant or Rent Administrator.
  3. Save. The user now sees the Rent Management app on their home screen.
  4. For property managers, open each of their properties and set them in the Property Manager field (chapter 6). Until you do, they see nothing.
Tenants are not staff users. They get portal access, which only shows their own lease, invoices and repairs. See chapter 8.
Chapter 04 · Getting started

First-time setup

Do this once, in this order, before you create the first lease.

  1. Company details and logo. Settings›Companies: name, address, logo, email, phone and bank account. They appear on every invoice, agreement and email.
  2. Outgoing email. Make sure Odoo can send email (your Odoo administrator sets up the outgoing mail server). Without it, tenants receive no invoices or reminders.
  3. Rent Management settings. Rent Management›Configuration›Settings (see the table below). The Security Deposit Account is required before you can invoice any deposit.
  4. Staff users and roles (chapter 3).
  5. Amenities (optional). Configuration›Amenities: lift, parking, power backup, Wi-Fi… These are tags you attach to properties, and tenants see them on the portal.
  6. Owners (optional). Tenants›Property Owners, if you manage buildings for other owners.
  7. Properties, then their units (chapters 6 and 7). Enter each unit's rent, deposit and meter number.
  8. Meter QR labels. For metered units, print the QR labels and stick them on the meters (chapter 18).
  9. Tenants, then their leases (chapters 8 and 10).
  10. Online payment (optional). To let tenants pay by card or UPI from the portal, your Odoo administrator enables a payment provider in Accounting›Configuration›Payment Providers.

Chapter 06 · Your portfolio

Properties

A property is a building or complex. It groups units and decides which manager is responsible.

Create a property

  1. Rent Management›Properties › New.
  2. Enter the name, type (Residential, Commercial or Mixed Use), the address and a photo.
  3. Choose the Owner (optional) and the Property Manager. The manager is the staff user who will see and run this property. It defaults to you.
  4. Tick the amenities and add notes if useful. Save. A code such as PROP/0001 is assigned automatically.

What the property screen shows

  • Buttons at the top: Units (all its units) and Leases (its running leases).
  • Occupancy: number of units, occupied, vacant, and the occupancy percentage. These update themselves.
  • The Units tab, where you can add units directly.
To stop using a property without losing its history, archive it (⚙ Action › Archive). Use the Archived filter to find it again.
Chapter 07 · Your portfolio

Units

A unit is what you rent out: a flat, room, shop, office or warehouse.

Create a unit

  1. Units › New (or from the property's Units tab).
  2. Name (e.g. A-101; must be unique within the property), property, floor, type, area, bedrooms, furnishing.
  3. Pricing: base rent, security deposit and monthly maintenance charge. These are copied into each new lease for the unit, and you can change them there.
  4. Electricity Meter No. if the unit has its own meter. New leases for it then default to metered electricity.

Unit status: set automatically

StatusWhen
VacantNo confirmed or running lease.
ReservedA lease is Confirmed but the tenant has not moved in yet.
OccupiedA lease is Active or Expiring.
Under MaintenanceYou ticked Under Maintenance on the unit. While it is ticked, no lease can be activated on it.

The unit also shows its Current Lease and Current Tenant, its Lease History and its repair requests.

Meter QR code

Every unit gets a QR code. Scanning it with a phone opens a page where staff enter the meter reading on the spot (chapter 18).

  • Print Label: prints the QR label to stick on the meter. To print many at once, select units in the list › Print › Meter QR Labels.
  • New QR Code: if a label is lost or copied, this issues a new code. Old labels stop working, so print and stick the new one.
Chapter 08 · Your portfolio

Tenants and owners

Tenants and owners are ordinary Odoo contacts with a few extra fields on the Rent tab.

Add a tenant

  1. Tenants›Tenants › New. The Tenant box is already ticked.
  2. Name, email (needed for invoices, reminders and portal access), mobile and address.
  3. Rent tab: emergency contact; KYC: ID type (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Other), ID number, KYC Verified, and scans of the ID documents.

The tenant's screen shows a Leases button and Rent Due, which is everything still unpaid on their lease invoices.

KYC data is only visible to Rent Management staff, never to the tenant or other users.

Give a tenant access to the portal

  1. Open the tenant and check the email address.
  2. Click ⚙ Action › Grant portal access, tick the contact and confirm.
  3. The tenant receives an invitation email with a link to choose a password.
  4. From then on they sign in on your website and land on their rent dashboard (chapter 23). They see a lease once it is Confirmed, never while it is a draft.

Owners

Tenants›Property Owners: contacts with Property Owner ticked. Pick them in the Owner field of a property.

Finding leases

In Leases, use the filters: Running, Draft, Confirmed, Expiring, Ended, Ending in 30 Days, Not Signed, Deposit Pending, My Properties. You can group by property, tenant, status or end month. The calendar view shows all lease end dates by month.

  • A unit cannot have two leases that overlap in time (Confirmed, Active or Expiring).
  • Only Draft or Cancelled leases can be deleted. All others stay for your records.
Chapter 10 · Leases

Filling in a lease

Leases › New. The system fills in a lot for you as you go.

Top of the form

FieldWhat to enter / what happens
TenantPick a tenant, or type a new name to create one.
UnitPick the unit. This copies the unit's rent, deposit and maintenance charge into the lease, and sets electricity to Metered if the unit has a meter number. The Property and Property Manager follow from the unit.
Start DateMove-in date. It also sets the billing day to the same day of the month (maximum 28) and, if empty, the End Date to one year later minus a day.
End DateLast day of the lease. Duration (months) is calculated for you.

Terms tab

  • Rent Amount: rent per month, even for quarterly or yearly billing (a quarterly invoice bills 3 × this amount).
  • Maintenance Charge: monthly society or maintenance charge, added to each rent invoice.
  • Notice Period: in days (default 30).
  • Escalation (%), Escalate Every (months): automatic rent increase (chapter 17).
  • Notes: extra terms and conditions. They are printed on the lease agreement.

Billing tab

  • Billing Frequency: Monthly, Quarterly or Yearly.
  • Billing Day: day of the month the rent is due (1–28).
  • Next Invoice Date: due date of the next invoice the system will create. It is filled in at move-in and moves forward after each invoice.
  • Late fees: type (Fixed Amount or Percentage of Due), value, and Grace Days (default 5) (chapter 16).
  • Electricity: Not charged, Fixed monthly amount, or Metered, with the rate, fixed charge and opening meter reading (chapter 18).

Other tabs and buttons

  • Deposit: deposit amount and status; after move-out, the settlement figures (chapter 11).
  • Invoices (accountants): every invoice of the lease with amount due and payment status.
  • Electricity: the meter readings (only for metered leases).
  • Maintenance: repair requests linked to this lease.
  • Documents: the Signed tick box and the Signed Agreement file. Attach the scanned signed copy with the paperclip in the chatter, then select it here. The tenant can then download it from the portal.
  • Smart buttons at the top: Invoices, Meter Readings, Maintenance.
  • The chatter on the right keeps the full history: status changes, emails sent, late fees, escalations, and your notes.

From draft to move-in

  1. Fill in the lease and click Confirm. The tenant receives the confirmation email with the Lease Agreement PDF.
  2. Optional: Request Signature notes in the history that a signature was requested. Get the agreement signed, attach the scan, and tick Signed in Documents.
  3. Accountant: Invoice Deposit (chapter 11).
  4. On move-in day: Activate (Move-in). Billing starts from here.
After confirmation, the unit and start date are locked. After move-in, the tenant, end date and rent are locked too. Check them carefully before confirming. To change the rent later, use escalation or a renewal.
Chapter 11 · Leases

Security deposit

The deposit is invoiced once, held on a liability account (it is not income), and settled at move-out.

  1. On a Confirmed or Active lease, an accountant clicks Invoice Deposit. An invoice for the deposit amount is created and posted. It is not emailed automatically; use Send on the invoice if you want to email it.
  2. When the tenant pays, register the payment on that invoice (chapter 15).
  3. At move-out, the deposit is returned through a credit note, after deducting what the tenant owes (chapter 13).
Deposit statusMeaning
PendingNot invoiced yet, or invoiced but not paid.
ReceivedThe deposit invoice is paid.
Partially RefundedSettled at move-out, with deductions.
RefundedSettled at move-out, refunded in full.
The deposit amount is locked once the deposit invoice exists. The Deposit Pending filter on Leases lists every lease still waiting for its deposit.
Chapter 12 · Leases

Renewing a lease

  1. On an Active, Expiring or Expired lease, click Renew.
  2. A new draft lease opens. It is a copy of the old one, starting the day after the old end date, for the same number of months. A link to the original is shown in Renewal Of.
  3. Adjust it if needed, for example the new rent or the end date, then Confirm. The tenant gets the new agreement.
  4. When the new term starts, click Activate (Move-in) on the renewal. The old lease becomes Renewed. If you forget, the night job marks it Renewed anyway once its end date passes, as long as the renewal is Confirmed.
The renewal does not ask for a new deposit (its deposit amount is 0). The deposit the tenant paid stays recorded on the original lease. Keep this in mind at the final move-out (chapter 30).
Chapter 13 · Leases

Move-out and settlement

One screen closes the lease, bills the final electricity and damages, and settles the deposit.

  1. Open the lease (Active, Expiring or Expired) and click Terminate / Move-out.
  2. In the window, check or enter:
    • Move-out Date
    • Damage Charges and a note explaining the deductions
    • Final Meter Reading (metered leases only; the last reading is shown for reference)
    The window calculates Unpaid Dues (all open invoices of the lease), Electricity Charges (readings not billed yet plus usage since the last reading) and the Refund Amount.
  3. Click Confirm Move-out.

What the system then does

  • Cancels any meter reading still "To Enter", records the final reading, and invoices all unbilled electricity.
  • Creates and posts a Damage Charges invoice, if you entered damages.
  • Creates a credit note for the full deposit and uses it to pay off the tenant's open invoices, oldest first.
  • Sets the lease to Terminated, frees the unit (it becomes Vacant), and records the settlement on the Deposit tab.
  • Emails the tenant the Move-out Settlement PDF and opens the credit note.

Worked example

Deposit held ............................................ 30,000.00
– Damage charges (broken window) ......................... –2,000.00
– Unpaid dues (March rent still open) ................... –15,000.00
– Electricity (4,700 – 4,520 = 180 kWh × 8.00) ........... –1,440.00
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Refund to the tenant ..................................... 11,560.00

The credit note of 30,000 pays the March invoice, the electricity invoice and the damage invoice. The 11,560 left on it is what you owe the tenant. To pay it out: open the credit note › Register Payment and record the bank transfer or cash you give back.

If the deductions are more than the deposit, the refund is 0, and the remaining dues stay open on the tenant's invoices for you to collect.
Chapter 14 · Money

Rent invoices

You don't create rent invoices by hand. The system creates them from the lease, before each due date.

When

Every night, the system looks for active leases whose Next Invoice Date (the due date) is within the next 5 days (the Invoice Days in Advance setting). For each one, it creates the invoice, posts it, emails it to the tenant with the PDF, and moves the next invoice date one period forward. If it missed some days (for example because the server was off), it catches up on every missed period, but it never bills beyond the lease end date.

What a rent invoice contains

LineAmountWhen
Rent unit – periodMonthly rent × months in the periodAlways
Electricity (fixed)Fixed amount × monthsLeases with Fixed Monthly Amount electricity
Maintenance chargeMonthly charge × monthsIf the lease has a maintenance charge
Electricity (reading details)kWh × rate, plus the fixed meter chargeConfirmed meter readings not billed yet

Invoice date = today (or the due date if earlier). Due date = the billing date. The invoice is linked to the lease and the property.

Kinds of lease invoices

In Billing›Rent Invoices, each invoice has a type: Rent Security Deposit Deposit Refund Late Fee Maintenance Damage Charges Electricity. Use the filters Rent, Deposits, Late Fees, and group by Property, Lease or Rent Invoice Type. You can't create invoices from this menu; they always come from a lease, reading or repair.

Bill now instead of waiting

On an Active lease, an accountant can click Generate Invoice Now. It creates the next period's invoice immediately and moves the next invoice date forward, exactly as the night job would.

Fixing a wrong invoice

  1. Open the invoice › Reset to Draft › Cancel.
  2. The system rolls back what the invoice did: its meter readings become billable again, and, if it was the latest rent invoice, the lease's next invoice date moves back to that period.
  3. Correct the lease if needed, then click Generate Invoice Now (or let the night job create it again).
A cancelled rent or electricity invoice cannot be brought back to draft, because that could bill the same period twice. Always generate a new one.
Chapter 15 · Money

Recording payments

Tenant pays you (cash, cheque, bank transfer, UPI)

  1. Open the invoice (from the lease's Invoices button or Billing›Rent Invoices).
  2. Register Payment: choose the journal (Bank / Cash), amount and date.
  3. The invoice shows Paid (or Partially Paid). Reminders and late fees stop for it.

Tenant pays online

  • If a payment provider is enabled (chapter 4, step 10), tenants click Pay now on the portal, or pay all overdue invoices at once.
  • The payment is recorded automatically and the invoice is marked paid.
  • Every invoice PDF also carries a QR code that opens the invoice online so the tenant can pay.

Receipt: on a paid invoice › Print › Rent Receipt. Full account history: on the tenant › Print › Tenant Statement (chapter 26).

Chapter 16 · Money

Late fees

Set on the lease, Billing tab: Late Fee Type, Late Fee Value and Grace Days.

  • Each night, every rent invoice still unpaid more than Grace Days after its due date gets one late fee.
  • Fixed Amount: the value as-is (e.g. 500). Percentage of Due: that % of the amount still unpaid on the invoice.
  • The fee is a separate invoice of type Late Fee, posted (not emailed automatically) and noted in the lease history with links.
  • Only rent invoices attract late fees. Deposits, electricity-only invoices and repair invoices do not.
Due 1 May · grace 5 days → no fee on 2–6 May → fee created on the night of 7 May (if still unpaid).
No late fees for a tenant: set the Late Fee Value to 0. Waive a fee already charged: reset that late-fee invoice to draft and cancel it, or issue a credit note.
Chapter 17 · Money

Rent escalation

On the lease, Terms tab: Escalation (%) and Escalate Every (months) (default 12).

  • At move-in, the next escalation date is set to start date + the number of months.
  • On that date, the night job raises the rent by the percentage (rounded to the currency), moves the next date forward by the same number of months, and writes in the history: "Rent escalated by 5% from 15,000 to 15,750."
  • Invoices created after that date use the new rent.
3-year shop lease from 1 Jan 2026, rent 40,000, +5% every 12 months
→ 1 Jan 2027: 42,000  → 1 Jan 2028: 44,100

The tenant sees the next increase on their portal, and the lease agreement PDF prints the escalation schedule.

Electricity billing

Three ways to charge electricity (lease › Billing tab)

ModeWhat happens
Not Charged / Included in RentNothing is billed for electricity.
Fixed Monthly AmountA fixed amount is added to every rent invoice.
Metered (Monthly Reading)The meter is read every month and billed by the unit (kWh × rate), plus an optional fixed meter charge. Enter the Opening Meter Reading at move-in.

The monthly reading cycle (metered leases)

1st of monthReading for last month created, status To Enter, and a to-do for the manager
→
YouEnter the current meter reading (on the phone or the computer)
→
ConfirmedkWh and amount calculated
→
InvoicedWith the next rent invoice, or straight away with Invoice Now
  • Previous reading is filled in automatically: the last confirmed reading of that meter, or the lease's opening reading.
  • A missed month is never skipped. The next reading simply covers the whole gap, because the meter keeps counting.
  • The current reading can't be lower than the previous one, and two readings of the same lease can't overlap in time.

Option A: on the phone, at the meter (recommended)

  1. Scan the QR label on the meter with the phone camera. Sign in to Odoo if asked; a Rent Management login is required.
  2. The page shows the unit, tenant, period, previous reading and rate.
  3. Type the current reading. Optionally take a photo of the meter, change the rate, and tick "apply this rate to the lease".
  4. Save. The reading is confirmed immediately. If no reading was waiting, one is created from the day after the last reading up to today.

Option B: on the computer

  1. Billing›Electricity Readings. The list opens on To Enter or Invoice.
  2. Type the Current Reading on each line and click the ✓ (or select several and click Confirm).
  3. To bill now instead of with the next rent: select confirmed readings › Invoice Now (accountants). This creates one electricity invoice per lease. It is not emailed automatically.

A reading can also be created by hand with New (choose the lease and the period). Readings can be reset to draft or cancelled until they are invoiced.

Maintenance requests

New→In Progress→Done  (or Cancelled → Reopen)

Where requests come from

  • Tenants report them on the portal (category, priority, description, up to 5 photos). The request appears on your board straight away and the tenant follows it online.
  • Staff create them in Maintenance › New. Choose the unit, and the current lease and tenant fill in by themselves.

Handling a request

  1. Set Assigned To (a staff user, who gets a to-do), the outside Vendor if any, and the priority (Low, Normal, High, Urgent).
  2. Start when work begins. The tenant sees "In progress".
  3. Enter the Cost and choose Charge To: Owner (you pay) or Tenant.
  4. Mark as Done. If it is charged to the tenant and has a cost, an invoice of type Maintenance is created and posted automatically.

Use the chatter to talk with the tenant: messages appear on their request page. Filters: Open, Done, Assigned to Me, High Priority, Charged to Tenant.

If the unit can't be rented during a big repair, tick Under Maintenance on the unit (chapter 7).
Chapter 20 · Operations

Automatic jobs (schedulers)

Seven jobs run every night, one after another. Each is safe to run more than once: it only does what is still due, so running a job twice never bills or emails twice.

Default times are shown in the server clock (UTC) and in India time (IST). You can change them (see below).

#Job nameRuns atWhat it doesWhat you see afterwards
1Rent: Prepare Monthly Electricity Readings00:15 UTC
05:45 IST		 For every running metered lease without a reading for last month, creates a reading To Enter from the day after its last reading to the last day of last month. In practice this happens on the 1st of each month. New readings in Electricity Readings; a to-do for the property manager due in 3 days.
2Rent: Expire Ended Leases00:30 UTC
06:00 IST		 Active or Expiring leases whose end date has passed become Renewed (if a renewal is confirmed or running) or Expired. Status changes on leases; units of expired leases become Vacant.
3Rent: Apply Rent Escalation00:45 UTC
06:15 IST		 Raises the rent of leases whose next escalation date has arrived, and sets the next date. New rent on the lease, and a note in its history.
4Rent: Generate Rent Invoices01:00 UTC
06:30 IST		 Creates, posts and emails the rent invoices due within the "Invoice Days in Advance" window (with fixed electricity, maintenance charge and confirmed meter readings), and catches up any missed periods. New invoices in Rent Invoices; "Rent Invoice" emails sent; Next Invoice Date moved forward.
5Rent: Apply Late Fees02:00 UTC
07:30 IST		 Charges one late fee on each rent invoice unpaid beyond its grace days. New Late Fee invoices and a note on the lease.
6Rent: Lease Expiry Alerts03:00 UTC
08:30 IST		 Active leases ending within the "Lease Expiry Notice" days become Expiring. "Lease Expiry Notice" email to the tenant; to-do "Lease ending: renew or plan move-out" for the manager.
7Rent: Payment Reminders and Overdue Notices06:00 UTC
11:30 IST		 Emails a reminder for unpaid lease invoices due within the "Reminder Days Before Due" window, and an overdue notice for unpaid invoices past their due date (if enabled). Each email is sent once per invoice. Emails in the invoice history.
The order is deliberate: leases are closed and rents escalated before invoices are created, so the invoices use the right status and amount.

Run a job now, pause it, or change its time

  1. Turn on developer mode (Settings, bottom of the page, Activate the developer mode).
  2. Settings›Technical›Automation›Scheduled Actions, and search Rent:.
  3. Open the job and:
    • Run Manually to run it right now;
    • switch Active off to pause it (switch it back on to resume);
    • change Next Execution Date to move the time it runs.
Emails are put in Odoo's outgoing queue and leave within a few minutes (Odoo's own "Mail: Email Queue Manager" job). If tenants receive nothing, check the outgoing mail server and that the tenant has an email address.

"How do I…?"

…rent out a vacant unit?
Units › filter Vacant › open it › Leases button › New (the unit is pre-filled). Fill in the tenant and dates, then Confirm, invoice the deposit, and Activate on move-in. See chapter 10.
…record a payment a tenant made in cash or by bank?
Open the invoice › Register Payment. See chapter 15.
…send an invoice to the tenant again?
Open the invoice › Send (or Print and share the PDF).
…give a tenant a receipt or a statement?
Receipt: invoice › Print › Rent Receipt. Statement: tenant › Print › Tenant Statement.
…bill this month's rent today instead of waiting?
Lease › Generate Invoice Now (accountants).
…correct an invoice with a wrong amount?
Fix the lease first (if it is still editable), then invoice › Reset to Draft › Cancel, then Lease › Generate Invoice Now. See chapter 14.
…change the rent of a running lease?
The rent is locked after move-in. It changes through escalation, or by renewing the lease with the new rent (chapter 12).
…handle a tenant leaving early?
Lease › Terminate / Move-out with the actual move-out date. See chapter 13.
…stop late fees for one tenant?
Lease › Billing tab › Late Fee Value = 0. To cancel a fee already charged, reset that late-fee invoice to draft and cancel it.
…charge a tenant for a repair?
On the maintenance request set Cost and Charge To = Tenant, then Mark as Done. The invoice is created automatically.
…take a unit off the market during repairs?
Unit › tick Under Maintenance. Untick it when it is ready.
…replace a lost meter QR label?
Unit › New QR Code › Print Label. The old label stops working.
…enter a meter reading without the phone?
Billing › Electricity Readings › type the current reading › ✓ Confirm.
…let a new staff member see only their buildings?
Give them the Property Manager role and set them as Property Manager on those properties. See chapter 3.
…give a tenant online access?
Tenant › ⚙ Action › Grant portal access. See chapter 8.
…change when invoices or reminders go out?
Configuration › Settings: Invoice Days in Advance, Reminder Days Before Due, Lease Expiry Notice. See chapter 4.
…make something happen now that normally happens overnight?
Run the scheduled job by hand. See chapter 20 and chapter 21.
…upload the signed lease agreement?
Attach the scan in the lease chatter (paperclip), then on the Documents tab tick Signed and select the file in Signed Agreement.
…see who has not paid?
Dashboard › Overdue tenants, or Rent Invoices › filter by payment status / overdue, or the tenant's Rent Due field.

Chapter 30 · Reference

Good to know

  • Rent is always per month, whatever the billing frequency.
  • Electricity from meters is billed one month behind, on the next rent invoice (or straight away with Invoice Now).
  • Every automatic email is sent once per invoice or lease. Running a job again never sends duplicates.
  • Cancelling a rent invoice puts its period and meter readings back in the queue to be billed again.
  • Request Signature only records the request in the lease history. No e-signature service is connected; signing is done on paper (or any tool you use) and the signed copy is uploaded.
  • WhatsApp / SMS are prepared but not connected; all notifications go by email today.
  • Deposit after a renewal: the deposit stays on the original lease, which becomes Renewed. Move-out on the renewal therefore settles no deposit. When a tenant who has renewed finally leaves, ask your accountant to refund the original deposit with a credit note on the original deposit invoice.
  • Multi-company: each company has its own settings, and staff only see the companies they have access to.
  • History is never lost: archive properties and units instead of deleting them; leases with activity can't be deleted.
